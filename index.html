ClaudeOS

ClaudeOS is a private assistant system used by Arenas Molina Group Ltd, its subsidiary companies and the Arenas Molina family. It is not a product or service, and it is not available to the public.

What it does

It helps the people who own the connected accounts with everyday administration: reading and organising email, preparing draft replies, keeping calendars in order, and creating or editing documents, spreadsheets and presentations in their own Google Drive.

It only acts on accounts whose owners have connected them themselves. Emails are sent only after the account owner has approved the exact message.
